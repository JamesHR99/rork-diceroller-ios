# Encounter wheel, sealed omens and the Ferryman

Ordinary stops now use a twelve-wedge prize wheel: eight Guardians, two Omens,
one Shrine and one Ferryman. Each wedge has the same probability and visible
size. The pointer stays still while the wheel makes five turns and eases to a
stop at the committed result. Accept with Sail Onward, or spend a path reroll.
A reroll can land on the same encounter type. Reduce Motion settles immediately
with a brief reveal delay. The opening practice guardian, heralds and
serpent-lords remain fixed and cannot be rerolled.

Path rerolls start at **1 per run**, are consumed individually, persist across
encounters, and cap at **3**. They are separate from battle dice rerolls. The
first spin at each ordinary stop is free. Saving during a spin preserves both
the committed result and its cost; resuming never gives another free spin.

Each omen retains its river story and deals three shuffled, sealed gifts. Only
the selected outcome is revealed; no choice costs health or gold. One of the
three grants a path reroll. Two others come from gold, healing, a face change,
a crit imbue, or a usable god boon. A reroll gift at the cap becomes 25 gold.
Selection is final and saved, including any pending die/face/boon targeting.

Every Ferryman visit has one of each consumable and two distinct class face
changes. All purchases cost gold:

| Stock | Gold | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Flask | 22 | Restore 30–60 health as the run progresses |
| Turn of Fate | 35 | Gain one path reroll, up to three |
| Face change (two offers) | 40 each | Choose a die and a face to change |
| God boon (25% of visits) | 90–130 | One eligible, unowned regular power |

Healing at full health and rerolls at the cap cannot be purchased. Each shelf
card sells once. Cancelling a purchased face change or boon replacement refunds
the gold and returns the card. Stock, purchases, pending targeting, hidden omen
gifts and revealed outcomes persist on save/resume.

Existing version-4 saves keep progress and gain the new one-charge purse when
no purse was saved. An unvisited legacy two-channel fork becomes one locked
wheel result on its first spin. Completed node identities are preserved.
